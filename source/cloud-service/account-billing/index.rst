Account and billing
===================

This section describes how to manage your personal account in Wazuh Console and how your organization pays for Wazuh Cloud environments and Wazuh CTI feed subscriptions.

-  :doc:`User settings </cloud-service/account-billing/user-settings>`: Edit your personal details, change your password, set up multi-factor authentication, and choose which notifications you receive.
-  :doc:`Payment methods </cloud-service/account-billing/payment-methods>`: Add, delete, and choose the default card of your organization.
-  :doc:`Subscriptions </cloud-service/account-billing/subscriptions>`: Review your subscriptions, pay pending amounts, and cancel or resume subscriptions.
-  :doc:`Invoices </cloud-service/account-billing/invoices>`: Review and download the invoices issued to your organization.
-  :doc:`Billing FAQ </cloud-service/account-billing/billing-faq>`: Find answers to common billing questions.

The **Billing** pages are available only to members with the Editor role.
