Your environment
================

A Wazuh Cloud environment is a full Wazuh XDR and SIEM stack that Wazuh hosts, updates, and operates for you. You create and manage your environments in Wazuh Console, under **Wazuh Cloud** > **Environments**, and you work with your security data in the Wazuh dashboard of each environment.

The following sections explain how to create an environment, change its settings, monitor its usage, and cancel it.
