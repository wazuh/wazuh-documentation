.. _cloud_support:

Support
=======

The **Help** group in the Wazuh Console sidebar gives you access to Wazuh support and to the Wazuh Cloud guides. This page explains the options on the **Support** and **Documentation** pages.

Support page
------------

To open it, in the sidebar, under **Help**, select **Support**. The options on the page depend on whether your organization has a paid environment or subscription.

Organizations with a paid environment or subscription
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  **Support Portal**: Select **Access Support Portal** to sign in to the `Wazuh Support Portal <https://wazuh-support.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/4>`__ and open a ticket with our engineers.
-  **Partner Portal**: If you are a Wazuh partner, select **Access Partner Portal** to sign in to the `Wazuh Partner Portal <https://partners.wazuh.com/>`__ for all matters related to Wazuh business.

To help us resolve your issue faster, include the following information in your ticket:

-  The Cloud ID of your environment. It is shown as **ID** in the **Information** card of the environment details page.
-  A clear description of the question or issue.
-  Steps to reproduce the issue, for incidents.
-  Relevant logs or screenshots.

Other organizations
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  **Support**: If your organization has a trial environment, active or ended, select **Contact us** to send a request to our team. See :ref:`Requesting assistance <cloud_support_request_assistance>`.
-  **Sales**: Select **Contact sales** to `contact our sales team <https://wazuh.com/contact-us/>`__ to request a product demo, information about support or training, or to become a partner.

.. _cloud_support_request_assistance:

Requesting assistance
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

#. In the sidebar, under **Help**, select **Support**.
#. Select **Contact us**.
#. In the **Request assistance** window, select the **Cloud environment** your request is about.
#. Describe what you need help with in **Message**.
#. Select **Send request**.

Wazuh Console shows **Your request has been sent. Our team will contact you by email.** While a trial is active, you can also send this request from the environment details page with **Manage** > **Request assistance**. You need the **Editor** role to use the **Manage** menu.

Documentation page
------------------

To open it, in the sidebar, under **Help**, select **Documentation**. The page links to the following guides:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Card
     - Content
   * - **First steps**
     - :doc:`Getting started </cloud-service/getting-started/index>`, from creating an account to monitoring your endpoints.
   * - **Enrolling agents**
     - :doc:`Enrolling agents </cloud-service/getting-started/enroll-agents>` in your environment.
   * - **FAQ**
     - The :doc:`Cloud service FAQ </cloud-service/getting-started/starting-faq>`.
   * - **Trial setup guide**
     - Step-by-step instructions to deploy and use your trial environment, on the Wazuh website.
   * - **Trial testing guide**
     - Use cases that show the Wazuh Cloud XDR and SIEM capabilities, on the Wazuh website.

Select **See more** on a card to open its guide.
