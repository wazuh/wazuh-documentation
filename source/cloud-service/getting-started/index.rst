Getting started
===============

Wazuh Cloud runs the Wazuh central components for you. We install, update, and scale them, so you can focus on securing your endpoints and responding to threats instead of managing infrastructure. You manage your organization, environments, and billing from **Wazuh Console** at `https://console.pro.cloud.wazuh.com <https://console.pro.cloud.wazuh.com>`__.

The pages in this section guide you from account creation to monitoring your endpoints:

#. :doc:`Create your account </cloud-service/getting-started/create-account>` in Wazuh Console and confirm your email address.
#. Get to know :doc:`Wazuh Console </cloud-service/getting-started/console-overview>`, its navigation, and the roles in your organization.
#. Create an environment. :doc:`Start a free trial </cloud-service/getting-started/start-trial>`, or :doc:`create a paid environment </cloud-service/your-environment/create-environment>` directly.
#. :doc:`Access the Wazuh dashboard </cloud-service/getting-started/access-wazuh-wui>` of your environment and change the default password.
#. :doc:`Enroll Wazuh agents </cloud-service/getting-started/enroll-agents>` on the endpoints you want to monitor.

If you have questions along the way, see the :doc:`Cloud service FAQ </cloud-service/getting-started/starting-faq>`.
