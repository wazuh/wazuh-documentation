CMMC levels
===========

CMMC has three levels:

-  **Level 1 (Foundational)**: the 15 basic safeguarding requirements of Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) clause 52.204-21 for FCI.

-  **Level 2 (Advanced)**: the 110 security requirements of NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 for CUI.

-  **Level 3 (Expert)**: the Level 2 requirements plus 24 requirements from NIST SP 800-172.

CMMC identifiers combine the domain, the level, and the NIST SP 800-171 requirement number. For example, AC.L2-3.1.5 is requirement 3.1.5 of the Access Control (AC) domain at Level 2.
