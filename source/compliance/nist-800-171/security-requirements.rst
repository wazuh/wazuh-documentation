NIST SP 800-171 security requirements
=====================================

Revision 2 of NIST SP 800-171 contains 110 security requirements in 14 families.

NIST published Revision 3 of SP 800-171 in May 2024. Revision 3 restructures the requirements into 17 families and uses new identifiers, such as 03.01.01. The DoD continues to require Revision 2 for DFARS clause 252.204-7012 and for the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) program.

Wazuh rules map findings to the requirement identifiers of Revision 2, such as 3.1.1 and 3.14.2.
